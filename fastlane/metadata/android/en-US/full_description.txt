One tap makes an Anki card on Android: the word, its reading and its definition, the sentence the word is in, a screenshot of the screen, the word audio and the sentence audio. The card goes into AnkiDroid on the device. Nothing leaves the device.

It works with any app. Select a word and choose "Add to Anki" in the text selection menu, share a text, or tap "Anki" in SubRead Dictionary or SubRead Overlay.

The definition, the reading and the word audio come from SubRead Dictionary, which reads Yomitan dictionaries. The sentence and the screenshot come from the capture service: an accessibility service that keeps the last text you select and a screenshot of that moment. The service is optional and off until you turn it on. The voice of the device reads the sentence.

The app makes a "SubRead" note type and deck on the first card. Any other note type works: the settings map each field to a part of the card.
